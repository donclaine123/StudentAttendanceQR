Quick Start Guide

EazyAttend allows you to manage classroom attendance efficiently using QR codes. Here's how to get started:

  1. Create your classes in the Classes section
  2. Generate a QR code for attendance in the Generate QR section
  3. Students scan the QR code with their devices
  4. View and export attendance records in the Attendance section
