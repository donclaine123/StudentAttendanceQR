Key Features

EazyAttend is packed with features to make attendance a breeze for both students and faculty.

Quick Scan & Go

Students simply scan a QR code upon entering class, marking their attendance instantly.

Detailed Reports

Teachers gain access to in-depth attendance reports, including attendance history, absences, and trends.

User-Friendly Interface

EazyAttend is designed for simplicity. Navigating the system is intuitive for all users, regardless of their technical expertise.

How EazyAttend Works

A simple 3-step workflow designed to save time for both educators and students.

Step 01 👩‍🏫

Teacher Generates QR Code

Instructor creates a unique QR code for each class, easily shared via screen display.

Step 02 📱

Students Scan & Check In

Students use the EazyAttend app to scan the QR code, registering their presence in seconds.

Step 03 💾

Automatic Attendance Records

Attendance is automatically recorded in a secure online database, accessible for reporting and insights.
